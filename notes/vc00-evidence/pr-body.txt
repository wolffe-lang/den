  Options: stop vendoring, or use implementation b603bb8 as formatted at
  f87dc76. Recommend/use the latter provisionally; provenance states it.

  fixed-Huffman encoder. Options: reproduce zlib's exact encoding, or require
  identical raw object bytes/oid plus interoperable zlib and measured size.
  Recommend the latter. No harness weakening implemented; vc01 draft is
  explicitly conditional on the adopted decision.

  defer push with all v0/v1, or allow traditional receive-pack framing for
  push while retaining v2-only fetch. Recommend the latter; no wire code yet.
