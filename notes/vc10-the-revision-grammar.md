### Recommendations implemented

- **R-vc10-1:** owned, bounded layers: 4,096-byte parser inputs, 256 suffixes, 10,000 numeric/ancestry counts, 64 object visits per peel and 256 path components. Suffix/path evaluation, candidate filtering and hint rendering each have a separate 128-MiB body-charge budget; bodies cap at 16 MiB. Prefix exploration caps at 65,536 nodes/256 identities; set arguments at 256 and inherited walks at 10,000 commits. These are payload/operation bounds, not peak-RSS or streaming guarantees.
- **R-vc10-2:** sets only where measured: log exclusions/two-/three-dot; standalone diff endpoints with one base for three-dot. Other operands stay single objects or explicit lists. Tracking, dates, searches, index selectors and parent-set shorthands refuse by name. Preserve all vc08/vc07 and M2 editor/display boundaries.

Pinned hint behavior is load-bearing: `log PREFIX` selects a unique commit-ish without a warning even when a blob shares its prefix; untyped rev-parse/cat-file remain ambiguous. Typed suffixes similarly constrain resolution. Hints use author/tagger dates, first-paragraph commit subjects or tag names and type-prioritized ordering. Refname ambiguity instead warns and follows documented six-level precedence. Required pseudorefs, parent order and raw paths retain provenance; no history read calls `index.write_tree`.
